# What I actually do before 9am

I wake at 6:10. Not by choice — the upstairs neighbour's shower pipe runs behind
my bedroom wall and it sounds like a jet spooling up.

For about four years I tried the thing where you journal for twenty minutes,
meditate for ten, and read something improving before touching your phone. I kept
it up for maybe nine days at a stretch. Then a deadline would land and the whole
scaffold would come down at once.

What stuck was smaller. Coffee, and one question written on an index card: what
is the one thing today that, if it doesn't happen, makes today a waste? I write
the answer down. Takes ninety seconds. Half the time the answer is boring, like
"call the dentist."

The index card works because it fails gracefully. A twenty-minute journaling
habit either happens or it doesn't. Ninety seconds happens on the bad days too,
which are the days it matters.

I still don't meditate. Tried it in 2019, gave it six weeks, got nothing out of
it that a walk didn't give me faster. Maybe I was doing it wrong. Plenty of
people I trust swear by it, so probably.

One warning. The index card turns into a to-do list if you let it, and then
you're back to managing a system instead of doing the work. When I catch that
happening I throw the cards out and start over the next morning with one line.
